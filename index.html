03 · About

Real experience.
Practical software.

Before moving into software development, I spent many years in manufacturing at Whirlpool and later operated an independent distribution business.

That work gave me firsthand experience with workflows, inventory, logistics, and customer needs. I saw how inefficient processes affect daily decisions and the people doing the work. That perspective shapes how I approach software: understand the problem and the people using it before choosing a solution.

Today, I focus on frontend development with React, Next.js, and TypeScript, building responsive, accessible applications that make practical tasks easier. My experience in operations and business helps me connect engineering decisions to what people need from a product.

What I bring to the work

Operations
An understanding of how workflows, inventory, and logistics fit together in day-to-day work.
Business
Experience balancing customer needs and daily decisions while running an independent distribution operation.
Engineering
A frontend focus that turns practical requirements into responsive, accessible interfaces.
